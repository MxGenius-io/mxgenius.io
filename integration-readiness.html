03 · Aviation response design

What should a correct answer look like?

Start with five high-value demo processes. Aviation experts can teach MXGenius the familiar order, required evidence, human decision point, and definition of done for each one.

Illustrative format only

Example: interpret a connected pressure reading

Values and limits below are placeholders. The real answer must use approved aircraft data and remain subject to authorized human review.

  1. ObservationState what was seen or measured, with unit and time.
  2. EvidenceName the device, record, manual section, and data currency.
  3. MeaningCompare the reading with the approved limit; say when evidence is missing or conflicts.
  4. Next actionGive the ordered technician steps, prerequisites, cautions, and stop conditions.
  5. Human decisionName who must inspect, approve, defer, or sign off. MXGenius does not release aircraft.
  6. RecordCapture the result, sources, device trace, owner, and disposition for the audit trail.
