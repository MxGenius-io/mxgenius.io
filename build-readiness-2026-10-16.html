XDrive V1: enclosure design and field readiness

Current stage: battery-based tapered enclosure remodeling. The goal is the first field-testable XDrive V1: an enclosed Pi/52Pi unit that plugs into its battery, connects to the intended network, and operates as an MXGenius appliance. The latest operator-marked photo proposes flipping/reorienting the arrangement and tapering the enclosure around the battery footprint. The owner is shaping that field enclosure; exact orientation and geometry remain to be established. The boxy R04 CAD is an earlier editable reference, not the selected V1 shape.

The October 7 design, slicing, and viewer work remains part of the history. The new photos and measurements replace the old side-by-side board and large wire-loop assumptions for this enclosure. They do not establish completed field acceptance.

Current hardware photos and design references

V1 field-test gate

  1. Finish the battery-based tapered enclosure. Resolve the flipped/reoriented stack arrangement and confirm the real board supports, overhead wire space, ports, icon/vent treatment, service access and cover retention.
  2. Verify the battery interface, grip and balance. Resolve central or offset anchor placement from the actual load path, weight distribution and feet footprint; match the footer, steel pocket, insulated hardware and screw lengths. Evaluate the proposed grip beneath the power input without blocking the plug or wire bend. Confirm reinforced carrying attachment, release/slide travel, secure retention and stable feet/bumper placement.
  3. Check the assembled power path. Record the installed components and protection; verify representative operation, secure wiring, thermal behavior and the disconnect/shutdown procedure.
  4. Prove networked operation. Start the battery-powered V1 unit, join the intended network, reach the MXGenius appliance/status workflow, and record image/service versions and observed health. Exercise reconnect and recovery.
  5. Record field evidence. Capture photos of the finished unit, the tested configuration, results, remaining defects and the decision to accept or revise. Until then, V1 field acceptance stays open.

Still open: final enclosure geometry, exact dock/plate fasteners, cover/latch retention, structural loading, cooling and the completed battery-powered network test. The R04 draft is not a validated field-ready or printer-ready V1.
